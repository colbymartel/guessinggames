Colby Martel
@ColbyMartel • Creator of GuessingGames.com

Growing up with social anxiety, YouTubers inspired me to go out of my comfort zone and make videos that inspire others. I started by showcasing the stories of Ivy League students, then started learning about people of all ages and backgrounds through fun guessing games.
