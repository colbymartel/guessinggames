HOW IT WORKS

From the audience to the stage.

You don't have to be selected before the show. Everyone gets a chance to compete.

STEP 01

Everyone Plays

A random category is revealed. The audience joins by QR code and has two minutes to answer as many questions correctly as possible.

STEP 02

Top Players Qualify

The three highest-scoring eligible players become the first contestants and take their places on stage.

STEP 03

One Winner Advances

Contestants race to guess a mystery word. The winner stays, while two new challengers qualify from the audience.
