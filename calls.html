Before you book

These calls are meant to be fun, positive, and comfortable for everyone. Booking a call means you agree to the rules below.

18+ may book directly

If you are 18 or older, you can schedule the call yourself through Calendly.

Ages 13–17: parent or guardian required

A parent or legal guardian must make the Calendly booking using their own name and email, approve the call, and be present for the call.

Under 13: not eligible

Calls are not offered to children under 13.

Calls are approximately 10 minutes

Please join on time. Late arrival may reduce the amount of time available and does not guarantee an extension.

Keep it appropriate

No sexual content, harassment, threats, hate, dangerous requests, illegal activity, or intentionally disruptive behavior. A call may be ended early for inappropriate conduct.

No recording without agreement

Do not record, livestream, screenshot, or repost the call without permission. If a call will be recorded or used in content, that requires separate consent.

Advice is informal

Creator, career, or life advice is based on Colby's personal experience and is not legal, financial, medical, mental-health, or other professional advice.

Make the call yours

Ask Colby to guess your name, favorite singer, birthday, or another fun fact — or spend the time talking content, creativity, social media, career goals, or whatever is most useful to you.

Parent / guardian consent for ages 13–17

The parent or guardian should make the Calendly booking themselves and attend the call. For an additional written record of consent, email bookings@colbymartel.com from the parent or guardian's email address with the minor's first name, age, booked date/time, and a statement confirming permission for the minor to participate.

Email Parent Consent
By booking, you confirm that the information provided is accurate and that you meet the applicable age and consent requirements. Separate written permission is required before any call is recorded or published.
